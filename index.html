About Laftah

A premium integrated event delivery company.

Laftah is designed for clients who need elegant delivery without operational ambiguity. Instead of managing separate vendors for planning, catering, production, technical support, rentals, and manpower, Laftah brings the full scope into one accountable delivery model.

Laftah creates the experience. OpsCore controls the execution.
