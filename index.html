                  Questions

                  Good to know.

                  What can I ask LAFTAH Experiences to deliver?

                  Events, hospitality, production, technical, rentals and crew. You can take all six together as Full Event Delivery, or choose only the services you need.

                  How does a project run?

                  Every project follows one structured workflow: scope, quotation, approvals, tasking, production tracking, crew deployment, live control and closeout reporting.

                  What should I include in my brief?

                  The occasion, date, location, guest count and the services you need. You can add the details gradually in the package builder, then send the brief to us.

                  Can I choose only some of the services?

                  Yes. Use the Custom tab in the package builder to switch services on or off, and refine each one if you wish.

                  How is pricing handled?

                  Each brief is scoped and quoted individually. The builder helps you describe what you need; we come back with a scoped quotation.

                  Is the page available in Arabic?

                  Yes. Use the language button at the top to switch between English and Arabic at any time.
